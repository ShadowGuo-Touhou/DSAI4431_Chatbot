Overview

The Starter fleets are a selection of fleets that come already pre-installed on your files on download of the game in their respective OSP and ANS folders. They are curated by selected community members as a way to give newer players a frame of reference for what the average 3000 point fleet looks like.

Keep in mind that all of the starter fleets are held to a certain standard and have to uphold certain requirements, meaning they cannot be fully optimized or take compromises in order to make them beginner-freindly. They are not designed to be meta or high end by most regards, but BY THEMSELVES serve well enough to be used in the standard multiplayer match.

ANS

TF Oak

Frontline Fleet: Dual Axford Heavy Cruisers optimized for winning gunfights against other heavy frontline assets.

TF Birch

Frontline Fleet: Double Vauxhall Light Cruisers with high-end missile backpacks and Sprinter EWAR Corvette.

TF Hemlock

Backline Fleet: Solo Axford Missile Cruiser.

TF Redwood

Frontline Fleet: 4TC Axford Heavy Cruiser backed up by dual Keystone Rail Destroyers and Sprinter Lockvette.

TF Ash

Ambush Fleet: Triple Keystone Beam Destroyer Array and an Sprinter EWAR Corvette.

TF Sycamore

Backline Fleet: Five ship Sprinter Bomber formation.

TF Maple

Backline Fleet: Solo Levy Escort Carrier backed up by dual Air Defense Raines Frigates.

TF Willow

Cap fleet: Three Keystone Beam Destroyers, Two Sprinter ELINT Corvettes, Three Capping Sprinters.

OSP

Cobalt Squadron

Frontline Fleet: Dual Ocello Command Cruisers optimized for winning gunfights against other heavy frontline assets.

Garnet Squadron

Frontline Fleet: Five ship Flathead Monitor formation optimized for fighting in plasma range and zoning ANS skirmish assets with bomb shell.

Kyanite Squadron

Frontline Fleet: Dual Marauder Line Ships optimized for winning gunfights against other heavy frontline assets and One LRT Draugr Tug

Zircon Squadron

Ambush Fleet: Dual Marauder Line Ships optimized for fighting in plasma range and One Sensor Draugr Tug

Azurite Squadron

Ambush Fleet: Solo Torpedo Cruiser backed up by One Sensor Draugr Tug and Two Rocket Shuttles.

Amethyst Squadron

Backline Fleet: Solo Moorline Carrier.

Wulfenite Squadron

Cap Fleet: Two Journeyman Light Carriers and Three Capping Ferryman Shuttles.

Tantalum Squadron

Cap Fleet: Tantalum is designed to engage in scout warfare. Scouting and killing scouts with a dispersed network of Draugr Multi-Mission Tugs and Ferryman Shuttles while utilizing a Flathead Monitor as a cruise missile platform against isolated targets of opportunity.
