The caliber comes from each cannon's Ammo field (Ballistic/Chemical - 450mm). Dimensions are the footprint the mounting socket must accept: compare them to the socket table of the hull (sockets/<hull>.md); a part fits when every dimension fits after rotating around the vertical axis.
