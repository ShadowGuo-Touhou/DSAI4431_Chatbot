# Catalog: parts exclusive to Shelter Alliance (SA) (54 parts)

Faction rule taken from the official wiki tables: a part whose name carries a faction roundel is restricted to that faction's hulls — these can only be fitted on SA hulls. Two roundels means both factions. **No roundel means the page does not restrict the part**, so it is available to both; across the whole wiki 203 parts carry no faction marking.
