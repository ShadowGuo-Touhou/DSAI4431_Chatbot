## 舰级要求（Ship Builds.txt 原文，作为判定依据）

**舰级硬性要求（required：缺一条就不算这个舰级）**
- have 2 250 or 450 turrets
- have 1 large VLS or CLS (mount 1-3)

**良好标准（should：缺了算有瑕疵，但仍算这个舰级）**
- have 2 450 turrets (on mount 1,3)
- have at least 1 Missile Programming Bus / Missile Programming Bus Array
- have > 500 pts of offensive missiles
- have hardkill or softkill
- have bullseye
- have good DC (at least 4 restores)
- have at least 2 CIC
- have at least 2 Drives
- have at least 3 ammo elevators

**可选加分（optional）**
- have 1 parallax radar or spyglass radar
- have Track Correlator if using spyglass radar
- have 1 ES32 Missile ID
- have extra antenna

**避免（avoid）**
- have rail turret
- have 250 turret
- have 120 turret
- have a beam turret
