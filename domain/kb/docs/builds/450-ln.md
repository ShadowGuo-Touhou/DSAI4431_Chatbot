## 舰级要求（Ship Builds.txt 原文，作为判定依据）

**舰级硬性要求（required：缺一条就不算这个舰级）**
- have at least 1 450 cannons

**良好标准（should：缺了算有瑕疵，但仍算这个舰级）**
- all cannons face the same direction
- have exactly 4 450 cannons total
- have good DC (include a large DC Storage and a Damage Control Complex)
- have at least 2 CIC and/or Auxiliary Steering
- have good softkill
- have at least 2 ammo elevators
- have at least 2 Rapid-Cycle Cradle

**可选加分（optional）**
- have 2 ammo storage
- have hardkill
- have T20 turrets
- have extra antenna
- have at least 1 Container Stack Launcher
- ^have Decoy Container and/or killjoy container

**避免（avoid）**
- having more or less cannons than 4
- have 250 cannons
- have C30 cannons
- have plasma cannons
- have mass drivers
- have mls launchers
- have turret on main mount  (mount 1-8)
