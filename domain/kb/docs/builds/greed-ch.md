## 舰级要求（Ship Builds.txt 原文，作为判定依据）

**舰级硬性要求（required：缺一条就不算这个舰级）**
- have at least 2 450 turrets (mount 1,3)
- have at least 3 120 turrets

**良好标准（should：缺了算有瑕疵，但仍算这个舰级）**
- have good softkill
- have at least 1 floodlight illuminator
