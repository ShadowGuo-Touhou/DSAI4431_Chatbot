# Munitions & Missiles › Point Defense

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
These munitions are specifically made for defensive work, to destroy targets such as craft, missiles, and some light ships.

Missiles are the most powerful weapons a ship can carry but they have limited ammunition, can be shot down and fire slower than other weapons. The number of missiles that can be prepared to fire at the same time depends on the ship's missile programming channels.
