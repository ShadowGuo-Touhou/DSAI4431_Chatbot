## Body
The Point Defense Missile Settings (PDMSL) are settings which control the behaviour of ships' and formations' AMMs and can override the AMMs' defensive targeting doctrines. The default values for these settings can be changed in the Settings screen off the Main Menu. These settings are abbreviated when displayed in the ship's control console (e.g. Fire Mode DCTRN / Fire Policy 1 Salvo / Salvo Size 1 / Last Salvo Yes will be displayed as D 1 1 Y).

The fire mode controls the logic that governs automatic launching of AMMs.

- DCTRN (D): The AMMs will launch automatically according to their defensive targeting doctrine.

- ALL (A): The AMMs will be indiscriminately launched against all nearby threats.

- MAN (M): AMMs will only launch against prioritized threats (ones the player has 'Z'+clicked on).

The fire policy controls the number of salvoes launched against a threat (not including last salvo).

- 1 Salvo (1): One salvo will be launched.

- 2 Salvo (2): Two salvoes will be launched.

- MAX DOF (M): Follow-up salvoes will be launched after the previous salvo's India time (time to intercept) has elapsed until the threat is destroyed.

Salvo size controls the number of AMMs launched in a single salvo against a threat.

- 1 (1): One AMM will be fired pre salvo.

- 2 (2): Two AMMs will be fired per salvo.

- 3 (3): Three AMMs will be fired per salvo.

- DCTRN (D): The AMMs' salvo size setting will be followed.

Last salvo controls whether or not a final salvo of missiles will be fired at the last possible moment regardless of fire policy or if other AMMs are in flight.

- YES (Y): A last salvo will be fired.

- NO (N): A last salvo will not be fired.

This setting appears to have been removed in favor of the NEZ setting in the carriers update
