Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Decoy containers appear as a ship, similar to the effects of a masquerade. These missiles are given a randomly generated name and take fleet prefixes friendly ships when being identified by enemy intelligence. These containers also emit radar signals that appear similar to a search radar which will show up on ELINT and attract ARAD seekers.

Decoy containers have 150hp, 2.0cm wall thickness, and unlike most other missiles, decoy containers can be hit and damaged by all weapons and projectiles.[6]
