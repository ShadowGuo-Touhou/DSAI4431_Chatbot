# Munitions & Missiles › Missile Construction › Wall Thickness

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Missile wall thickness is armor for the missile, though unlike ship armor, missiles use a far simpler system. If the AP of the incoming attack is less than the wall thickness, it will only deal half damage to the missile. It is measured in centimeters, i.e. 0.2 wall thickness is 0.2cm or 2mm.
