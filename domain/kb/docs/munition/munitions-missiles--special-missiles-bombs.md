## Body
Bombs are an unguided dumb munition meant to either finish or disable ships. Mainly used by spacecraft such as fighters and bombers as finisher weapons, they utilize their high damage fragments similar to mines to break components with a high high damage resistance and damage threshold. Bombs have fratricide, meaning they detonate when destroyed, chain-reacting with other bombs in the radius. Despite being in the bomb category, FBU-15 behaves like a rocket.
