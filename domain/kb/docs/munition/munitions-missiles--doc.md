# Munitions & Missiles

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Missiles and Munitions comprise the physical weapons fired and thrown around from both factions. From dedicated anti-ship missiles and Anti-Missile Missiles, to capital-grade shells and energy projectiles. Each faction has their own specific ammo types, but a lot of ammo types and calibres are shared between both.

Munitions comprise mainly of unguided shells with a specific calibre attributed to them. Unlike their missile counterparts, these weapons are unable to be decoyed or shot down, and have hardset statistics like speed and range.

Some, but not all, craft can share munitions with ships, although this is limited to only a few calibres.
