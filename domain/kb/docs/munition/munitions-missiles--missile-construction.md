Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Missiles can be designed and colored in the fleet editor. If a design is deleted it will be removed from all missile launchers in the fleet.

Selecting a missile's engine will show the engine configuration triangle, allowing the engine to be focused on Top Speed, Burn Duration or Maneuverability.

Note that some of the missile body stats are exaggerated, specifically engine tunings like max speed. While it is possible to make a 15km range sdm-1, you will most likely be on the lower end or middle of the spectrum of range. These statistics where made with EXTREME values. The missile body cost has engine cost considered as well, as while the SGM-H-200 Cyclone has a missile body cost of 3, the actual minimum cost is closer to 5 with no components added.
