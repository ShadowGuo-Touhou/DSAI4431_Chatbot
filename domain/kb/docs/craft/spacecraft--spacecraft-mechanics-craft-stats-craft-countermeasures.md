## Body
A pattern is a string of chaff packets dropped from under a craft as it flies. The packets form one decoy cloud that traces the craft's path. Every craft has a Countermeasures socket that holds one of three launchers.

|  | Standard CM Launcher | High-Capacity CM Launcher | High-Coherence CM Launcher |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost (pts) | 0 | 2 | 2 |
| Pattern Count | 4 | 8 | 4 |
| Shots Per Pattern | 10 | 10 | 20 |
| Shot Spacing | 0.25 s | 0.25 s | 0.125 s |
| Time to lay a pattern | 2.25 s | 2.25 s | 2.375 s |
| Packet lifetime | 6 s | 6 s | 6 s |
| Cloud lifetime | 8.25 s | 8.25 s | 8.375 s |
| Radar return multiplier | ×1 | ×1 | ×2 |
| Wake strength | 100 | 100 | 200 |
| Sets off missile fuzes | No | No | Blast-frag warheads, 33% per contact |

The second to fourth rows use the in-game stat names. Each shot drops one chaff packet. The game shows the High-Coherence spacing as 0.13 s; the exact value is 0.125 s.
