† The radar's maximum range is reached first. The game adds half the target's length to the maximum, so these targets are seen up to 55 m beyond it.

- Craft radars reach 3 km (fighters) or 4 km (Halberd, Claymore, Sturgeon). A 3 km craft radar sees every craft out to its full range. A 4 km one falls slightly short against the Tanto, Pike and Claymore from some angles (3.7–4.0 km).

- Against small missiles craft radars fall well short. A 4 km craft radar sees a Balestra-size missile at 2.0 / 2.6 / 2.8 km.

- The E-44 Sundial's 10 km radar sees a Tanto at 8.5 / 8.7 / 9.95 km.
