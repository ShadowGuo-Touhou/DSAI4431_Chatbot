# Damage Control › Debuffs/Catastrophic Events › Rare Debuffs/Catastrophic Events › Magazine/Cell Cookoff

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Magazine/Cell Cookoffs can appear on magazines and VLS cells. If 30 points of repair work is not completed within 80 seconds, an internal explosion occurs around the hit position that triggered the cookoff. It distributes up to 3000 component damage per batch among components within 50 m, processed in batches of up to 20 components and reduced by hull Component DR. The armor damage uses brush size 6, corresponding to about a 15 m radius on current hulls. When a Magazine Cook-off is in progress, you can hear crackling sound effects in the vicinity of the ship.

Container bank cookoffs require 60 points of repair work within 120 seconds instead, and deal 15000 damage (reduced by each part's component DR) split evenly among up to 20 parts at a time within 75 m of the launcher; the game repeats this for each further batch of up to 20 parts in range. Armor is stripped by 30 brush hits of size 10 (about 25 m radius each on current hulls) spread along the container bank, not by one 100 m sphere.
