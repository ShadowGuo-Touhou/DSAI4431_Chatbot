# Electronic Warfare › Notes

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
- ↑ THis is sorted by the effective jamming power on target, not the raw output of the jammer.

- ↑ The total assumes all jammers are the same type, at the same range, if combining jammers from multiple different distances or different types, refer to the multiplier per jammer.

- ↑ All vanilla jammers have an effect area ratio of 0.4, except for reactor blooms which have 1.0 instead

- ↑ The amount of jammers that can be combined this way is explicitly hardcapped to 20 strongest jammers, likely for lag reduction reasons, but the increase is already less than a hundreth of a percent after the 8th jammer

- ↑ Technically, all directional ewar emitters including radar and comms jammers have an edge falloff stat, but all of them (besides the illuminators) have 0.0 edge falloff which means they have no falloff at the edge of their FoV

- ↑ A radar with the transmitter and receiver in the same location is referred to as a "monostatic" radar. Illuminators have the transmitter and receiver in different locations, creating a "bistatic" radar

- ↑ Since RCS is angle dependent, this uses the angle between the target and the radar. The illuminator angle is not used.
