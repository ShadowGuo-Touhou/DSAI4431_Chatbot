# Component Damage › Damage Mechanics › Damage Reduction

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
A Hull's Damage Reduction stat reduces incoming damage by the percentage shown. For example, 40% DR will reduce 100 damage to 60 damage.

This modifier applies before damage threshold checks, and therefore massively amplifies the protective effect of Damage Threshold, especially against smaller munitions.

DR is 25% of the hull's base component DR when 20% or fewer component slots are filled, then rises linearly and reaches its full value at 60% filled. Slot size does not matter, only the number of slots filled. Damage Reduction does not change as modules are destroyed (as these modules are still present in their destroyed state).

Damage reduction does not affect fire, bshort, or burnthrough damage. Some attacks ignore DR.
