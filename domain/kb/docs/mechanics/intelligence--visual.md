## Body
Smoke (right) released by a dead shuttle in the testing range.

Ships within visual range will have their model revealed, which can reveal their loadout and current status. The ship's bridge lights switch to blue to amber when the ship has no more power (eg, all reactors disabled or destroyed) External modules show black smoke and sparks when destroyed.
