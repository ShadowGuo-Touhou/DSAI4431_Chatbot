# Electronic Warfare › Radar Illumination

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Radar illumination works in the game by painting targets inside the cone and increase their return power. Illuminators work similarly to jammers when it comes to calculating power on target over range, but instead the power is used to boost return power for all radars (search FC, and missiles radars). It is often used as guidance for SARH(Semi Active Radar Homing) missiles, but can also aid in radar searching and locking.
