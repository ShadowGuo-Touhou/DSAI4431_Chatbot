# Electronic Warfare › Sensor Jamming

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Sensor jamming attempts to bombard the opponent's radars with random noise to prevent tracking of friendly assets. Ships know when they are jammed and most search radars provide a jamming Line of Bearing (LoB). The R400 Bloodhound, the R550 Early Warning Radar, and fire-control radars do not. As such, this does not completely prevent the enemy from detecting your presence. However, jamming can be instrumental for reducing situational awareness of the opponent and preventing accurate fire on friendly assets.
