# Damage Control › Debuffs/Catastrophic Events › Rare Debuffs/Catastrophic Events › Fuel Line Fires

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Fuel Line fires can appear only on drives. A drive can also roll Reactor Overload, because drives are powerplants; a reactor cannot roll Fuel Line Fire. If 40 points of repair work is not completed within 80 seconds, it will cause multiple small purple explosions to erupt across the ship, dealing heavy damage across the ship

it specifically causes 2 to 4 explosions per 10 m of bounding radius, rounded up (2n through 4n-1, where n = ceil(half the longest select-box side in 10 m units)),[4] e.g. a Sprinter suffers 8-15 explosions and a Solomon 28-55.

Each explosion appears in a random position inside the hull and deals 200 damage in a 15m radius, and shreds armor in a 15m radius. Explosions happen every 0.2-0.5 secs until the amount of explosions are reached.
