# Intelligence › Sound › Damage

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Visible ships have a chance to play a certain sound effect when some of its components are directly damaged (hits on structure before structurebreak will not play this sound). This chance starts at ~1.50% per hit, increases to ~3.00% at 130 damage, ~50% at 300 damage, then increases by 0.0317% per point of damage past 300 damage

The sound effect can only play once every 2.5 seconds, and will only play if the model is visible.

If a ship gets structurebroken, a damage sound effect is played immediately, ignoring the above restrictions.
