# Component Damage › Damage Type Overviews › Plasma

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Plasma splits its damage across all components in its blast radius.

Plasma no longer casts additional splash rays (splash ray count is 0). Armor shredding comes only from the main hit.

Plasma armor shredding ignores armor angle. Both component damage and shredding falls off over distance, down to 30% at max range.
