## Body
Further information: component damage: overpenetration

If a shell is able to penetrate and does not stop inside the hull, it makes another check to penetrate the armor on the other side to exit the hull. The armor penetration is reduced by the distance traveled through the ship's interior density[needs confirmation]. If the second penetration check succeeds, the round overpenetrates and does reduced damage.
