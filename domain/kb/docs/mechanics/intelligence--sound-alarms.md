# Intelligence › Sound › Alarms

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Upon being destroyed, a ship sounds an evacuation alarm. If the ship is visible, an evacuation announcement follows about 7.5 seconds later. Lifepods begin launching 45 seconds after the alarm, one every 2.5 to 7.5 seconds.
