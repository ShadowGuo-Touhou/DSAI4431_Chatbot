# Component Damage › Damage Type Overviews › AP (Shell)

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
AP shells damage the first non-destroyed component in their path; all subsequent components along it's pendepth are not damaged. If they overpen, the reduced damage is spread equally among all components that the round intersected with. Grapeshot as well as individual fragments from bomb and RPF shells act like AP shells.
