# Component Damage › Damage Type Overviews › RPF (Shell)

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
RPF shells fire multiple fragmentations in a tight cone towards the nearest target (simulating the few fragments that would potentially threaten the target, out of the dozens of fragments). Each fragment acts like an AP shell, and totals up to the listed damage (eg, 250mm HE-RPF Shells deal 16 damage per fragment, for a total of 128 damage).

Most RPF shells ignore normal penetration depth mechanics; if the fragment penetrates armor, they will always penetrate a certain depth into the hull regardless of interior density or leftover penetration.

RPF will also fuze on missiles and craft, and has a 50% chance of damaging missiles and craft within its blast radius (20 damage, 0.1cm pen, 37.5m fuze, 110m radius for 120mm RPF; 30 damage, 0.2cm pen, 45m fuze, 200m radius for 250mm RPF). This blast radius is purely antimissile/craft and does not affect ships.

RPF will trigger on terrain.
