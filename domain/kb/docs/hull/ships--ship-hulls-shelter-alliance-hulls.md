# Ships › Ship hulls › Shelter Alliance hulls

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Shelter Alliance ships scale linearly. Larger ships are more resilient and carry more components but are slower and more expensive.

Worden Fleet Carrier and Mercator Replenishment Oiler are hidden from the skirmish fleet editor. They show up when editing a campaign fleet, or after the FleetEditorShowHidden console command, which tags them HIDDEN.
