# Ships › Integrity rating

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
A ship's integrity directly affects how resistant its components are to damage. Lots of empty space means less structural members to absorb damage and protect components. A ship with at least 60% of its slots filled (mounts, compartments, and modules) gets the full hull Damage Reduction. From 60% down to 20% fill the protection falls linearly, and at 20% fill or less it is 25% of the hull value.
