## Body
The Mk550 Railgun and Mk600 Beam Cannon are 4x12x4 and fit the Keystone's 4x12x4 mount. The TE45 Mass Driver is 4x10x4 and fits Protectorate mounts at least that large, including Marauder 6x10x6 mounts and the Flathead 6x12x6 mount. All three are offensive and have an optical backup.
