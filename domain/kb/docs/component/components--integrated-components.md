# Components › Integrated Components

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Integrated Components are found on all ships, have no cost, and are un-customizable and irremovable. They come in 7 types: Antennas, Radar panels/arrays, Landing Pads, Replenishment Docks, Main Thrusters, Braking Thrusters, and Maneuvering Thrusters.

Values are each part's base stats in game version 0.6.2.6. Lateral Power is a thruster's share of the ship's thrust, not a force. Radar panels and arrays set which directions the ship's radar can see; its range and power come from the radar component fitted.

No Integral component is reinforced. Mains and Braking thrusters are the only thrusters that receive increased thrust on flank. Mains are the only thrusters that generate a wake root signature when active. Non-main non-braking thrusters are also used for angular rotation; though neither their angular thrust nor the ship's maximum rotation speed are affected by the condition of these thrusters. DC priority of all thrusters is low. HP to function of all thrusters is 10.
