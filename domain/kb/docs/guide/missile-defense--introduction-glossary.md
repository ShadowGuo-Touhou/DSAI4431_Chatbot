# Missile Defense › Introduction › Glossary

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
- A

  - ACT - Short for Active Radar Homing

    - EACT - Short for Extended ACT

    - SEACT - Short for Steerable Extended ACT

  - Active Decoy - A size 2 defensive decoy found on ANS ships which emit both ARAD and ACT signatures.

  - ALLSTOP - A movement command accessable to ships, which causes them to slow down to 0 m/s

  - ARAD - Anti-Radiation, a type of missile seeker that looks for radar emissions.

- B

  - Bellbird - OSP directional radar jammer.

  - "Ball"" - Community term for the E70 'Interruption' Jammer.

- C

  - CCK - Ocello Killer, uses the abbreviation for "Command Cruiser" (CC) in the beginning. A family of missiles that are commonly made specifically to kill bow-on ocellos. Commonly comprised of salvoes of 3x HEKP S3H missiles using common straight-flying seeekers like CMD/Backup and ACT/[EO] Reject. Refers to a specific archetype of HEKP S3H missiles that are typically seen on double Vauxhall fleets such as TF Birch, VLS-3 backpack capitals, or sometimes off of yub fleets. CMD/Backup for direct, ACT/[EO]r, EO/HOJ and other blindfire cruise missile seeker combos for yub. They are commonly set up to support eachother by staging in a certain way where they converge and share damage taken from Auroras.

  - Chaffbox - Common term for a VLS-1 launcher with chaff.

  - CMD - Command Missile Seeker.

  - CMDball - See "Ball"

- ↑ automatic launches are limited (about one per launcher per second on VLS-2 and per 0.25 seconds on VLS-1, and at most five new targets per missile group per defense pass)

- ↑ With a BF-EL warhead, AMMs usually damage a missile (about 65% in a crossing intercept and 85% head-on). Plain blast fragmentation is about 20% crossing and 50% head-on, and a failed roll does no damage to a missile.
