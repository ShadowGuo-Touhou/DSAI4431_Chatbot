# Beginner's Guide › Damage control › Scramming

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Initiating a reactor scram can be done at any time and requires that you hold down the button until the progress bar completes, which takes about 3 seconds. This confirmation process can be skipped by pressing space while holding down the button, jumping the camera into the tactical map and immediately initiating the scram. The reactor scramming process takes 30 seconds and completes even without a DC team present. Power and mobility for the entire ship will be disabled for the duration of the process and 30 seconds after until these systems are brought back online. Scramming will only grey components that were over-loading before the process completed, other systems will be left intact. A general rule of thumb is that you should scram the reactor if you have 4 or more crits on it, as these crits are likely to slow down the progress of your DC teams. Special attention should be payed on larger ships to how long the DC teams are taking to actually reach the reactor in question as well as the status of any DC buffing components like Redundant Reactor Failsafes, Damage Control Central, Small Workshop, and Damage Control Complex, which do not provide their bonuses when they are below their functioning threshold; you may have less time than you expect to repair the reactor. Also be sure to check the current status of the powerplant components(s) in question about 40 seconds before detonation to determine if additional crits have been applied by enemy fire, ongoing fires, or other events in the minute or so since you last checked. If so, what was once a save-able overload may no longer be be saveable; Scramming will be necessary.
