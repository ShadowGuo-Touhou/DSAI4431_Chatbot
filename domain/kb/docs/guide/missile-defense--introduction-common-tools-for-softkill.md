# Missile Defense › Introduction › Common tools for softkill

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
There are multiple different tools in your arsenal to softkill effectively. Using them and having them available on your ship is key to practicing softkill.

VLS-1

- Chaff (For ACT and SAH, creates a large radar signature)

- Flares (For WAKE/THERM Primaries)

VLS-2

- Conure Active Decoy (For ACT and ARAD, has a larger radar sig. than conventional chaff alongside releasing pARAD signatures.

- Size 2 Defensive "Killjoy jammer decoy" Missile (Cruise or Direct) (For ACT, SAH, and ARAD. has a Self Screening Jammer which allows it to decoy and attract ARAD and jam out ACT and SAH)

Container Launcher

- Container Decoy (For ACT and ARAD. The Line Ship container has a large radar return, about 32,000-80,000 m². The Clipper container is about 1,600-4,000 m², smaller than chaff. Both emit pARAD at strength 1,000, far below the Conure.)

- Helljoy container missile (For ACT, SAH, and ARAD. has a Self Screening Jammer which allows it to decoy and attract ARAD and jam out ACT and SAH. Mainly fired at Claymore bombers in transit to attempt to also kill them.)

Interrupt CMD Jammer/Hangup/Warbler

- Mainly for jamming out CMD missiles

Floodlight/Lighthouse Illuminator

- Mainly for Self-Illuminating chaff clouds, can also counter radar jamming.

Radar jammer (Blanket, Bellbird, Lyrebird)

- Mainly for jamming out ACT and SAH missiles, be wary of its ability to attract ARAD and HOJ.

Blackjack Dazzler

- Mainly for jamming out EO missiles.
