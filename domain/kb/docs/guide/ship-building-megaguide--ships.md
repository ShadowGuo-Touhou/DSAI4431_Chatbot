# Ship Building Megaguide › Ships

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Ships are the fundamental unit of Nebulous: Fleet Command, coming in a variety of different basic hulls that may be further customized to the user's liking.

Note, the Starter Fleets in the Starter_Fleet_Guides were generated and maintained by the community and are designed to be competitive in multiplayer. It is recommended to review those ships build in the fleet editor to see some of the design philosophies presented below in action.
