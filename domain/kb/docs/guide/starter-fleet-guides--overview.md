# Starter Fleet Guides › Overview

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
The Starter fleets are a selection of fleets that come already pre-installed on your files on download of the game in their respective OSP and ANS folders. They are curated by selected community members as a way to give newer players a frame of reference for what the average 3000 point fleet looks like.

Keep in mind that all of the starter fleets are held to a certain standard and have to uphold certain requirements, meaning they cannot be fully optimized or take compromises in order to make them beginner-freindly. They are not designed to be meta or high end by most regards, but BY THEMSELVES serve well enough to be used in the standard multiplayer match.
