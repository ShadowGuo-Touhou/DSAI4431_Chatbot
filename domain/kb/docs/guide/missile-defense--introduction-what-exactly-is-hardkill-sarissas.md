## Body
Sarissas are a little bit of an odd-ball, they are an extremely accurate and long ranged point defense turret being able to fire high velocity rounds with a high level of accuracy. As good as they are, they fail to effectively kill off large numbers of missiles and craft. They are also extremely expensive and power-hungry. They are frequently countered by players by simply setting their craft and missile to evade manually, either through HBURN EVADE or by simply going in a zig-zag pattern.
