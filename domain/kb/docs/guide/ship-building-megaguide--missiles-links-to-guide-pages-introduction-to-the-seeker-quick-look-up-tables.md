## Body
Below you will see quick reference tables of popular Missile Seeker Combinations courtesy of Kate. Note, there are other combinations of Seekers as this is not an exhaustive list of options.

Nomenclature

- A Seeker such as "ACT" is a Tracking Seeker. It will track the target.

- A Seeker in brackets such as "[ACT]" is a Validation Seeker. It will validate the tracking seeker's target and make the primary seeker reject targets it wouldn't have otherwise ignored. If the Track Seeker is disabled the missile will go wild (such as ACT if it is undergoing RADAR Jamming).

- A Track Seeker with a r or ! such as "ACTr" or "ACT!" are set to Reject targets that aren't validated.

Some Missile combinations are identified as making the missile a "StraightRunner" when they do not detect a target they will continue on the course it was last on which may impact the target. These are identified in the table below. For additional details please see Missile_Defense page.
