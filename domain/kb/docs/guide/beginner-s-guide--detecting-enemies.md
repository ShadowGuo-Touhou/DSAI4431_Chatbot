# Beginner's Guide › Detecting enemies

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Enemy ships can be detected by active radar, by visual observation at short range, and passively by picking up their radar emissions (ELINT bearing lines and crossfixes). Both can be obstructed by asteroids.

- Radar detection is shared across the entire fleet as long as a ship's communications are online and allows all of a ship's weapons to fire on a detected target. Each ship has a number of radar panels that give full coverage around the ship. Radar panels or the radar controller can be destroyed, impairing radar functionality. Radar can also be jammed.

- Visual detection is not shared across the fleet. It uses a 500 m grid and a 5-cell sight stat (2500 m nominal): depending on direction and where the ships sit in their cells, contact starts reliably near 2.7 km and can extend to about 4.4 km. Enemies detected by visual will only be visible if the ship detecting them is selected and cannot be attacked by other ships unless those also detected the enemy. Guns, beams, and unguided rockets can fire on visually detected enemies if radar detection is not functional. Guided missiles cannot, including command-guided missiles: they need a non-visual track.
