## Body
As appealing as hardkill is to a newer player looking for an easy and cool-looking way to defeat missiles, they are easily countered by higher-level play. Hardkill trades into a certain number of missiles at a time, and doesn't scale well into large missile attacks (not even flak). They are easily countered by decoys, terminal maneuvers, evasion tactics, ect.

The only reliable way of countering almost every guided missile in the game, with a near 100% effectiveness if you set it up right, is found in the 2nd type of missile defense: Softkill.

Softkill is the practice of countering a missile by seducing, disrupting, or otherwise causing a missiles seeker to not go towards your ship or see it. They have the major advantage of countering a missile not through health depletion, but rather directly causing the missile to intentionally miss your ship with more reliability than hardkill could ever dream of imagining. They aren't affected by penetration aids, missile type, speed, and salvo size: the only thing affecting softkill's effectivity is the seekers used and the tools you have on board. Unlike hardkill, softkill trades in better into missile salvoes by being able to counter entire salvoes at once, unlike hardkill which needs numerical advantage to kill a set number of missiles. Of course softkill isn't completely foolproof, you are unable to softkill unguided missiles and there's always a certain element of RNG involving missiles, meaning that to effectively defend yourself against missiles you need at least SOME point defense to fully protect yourself against all types of missiles. Generally, a full softkill suite + your choice of point defense covers almost all your bases.
