## Body
Conversely the alternative to "Hardkill" is "Softkill" this is the practice of using electronic warfare and deception to seduce the tracking systems of hostile missiles such that they will be unable to track your craft and miss it. Compared to the Hardkill above, this is far more difficult, complicated, and time sensitive compared to the hard kill systems that will automatically engage hostile inbound missiles.

Fear not, when done right and early enough Softkill can be 100% effective in seducing those pesky missiles away from your precious ships. In the next section you will learn about all of the different Softkill equipment that you will need combine in your fleet to defeat hostile missile threats.

One part of softkilling the CMD Family, or the “yellow seekers”, there are two jammers for each faction:

- E70 ‘Interruption’ Jammer for ANS and Ocello

- J75 ‘Warbler’ Jammer for OSP.

You can also use the E71 ‘Hangup’ Jammer on ANS for this task but it is not recommended due to it being a cone and not omnidirectional as CMD seekers tend to track the ship anywhere it is in the world. It does have a niche of remotely jamming CMD missile strikes coming to friendlies.

- No, Home-On-Jam and ARAD Seekers do not track command jamming for balance reasons.

Player-made decoy type that can help softkill if not completely a large number of seeker combos. There are multiple variants of the killjoy with differing functions and niches but the default one is all you need to know for now:

- Seekerless or WAKE Primary, No Warhead, Self-Screening Jammer support module.

- SGM-200 ‘Tempest’ and Container Missile Body

- SGT-300 ‘Pilum’ body can be used, you just need to minimize its maneuverability since torps cost more for more maneuverability, should cost the same as the Tempest.

Set missile matrix to defensive but don’t give it valid targets and don’t set your PDMSL computer to all. Then uses the Z key which is the PD priority key on the incoming enemy missile for it to launch immediately and skip programming time. For MLS Killjoys just launch them in a direction.

(There will eventually be a separate guide for different killjoys variants and more details on using them)

Conure (ANS only) and Container Decoy (OSP only) are used to defeat the ARAD Family or the “green seekers” and ACT/[WAKE] somewhat. They’re just big chaff, should be intuitive to use.

Conure fits in a VLS-2 is controlled by the DECOY computer. Container Decoy fits in container stack launchers and requires you to launch them manually.

EA12 Chaff, EA20 Flares, EA14 Chaff, fits in VLS-1, controlled by DECOY computer, should be intuitive to use.

It should be noted that some capital ship’s radar signatures are bigger than EA12’s radar signature, particularly: Axfords, Solomons, Ocellos, and Liners. For ANS, you may have Conure as a secondary decoy option and/or the ability to spam EA12. For OSP, you should upgrade to EA14 and/or have Liner Container Decoys as a secondary decoy option.

Using a E57 ‘Floodlight’ Illuminator and pointing it at your own chaff, this is to increase the radar signature of the chaff as well as softkilling SAH seekers.

The ANS has a unique seeker type called Electro Optical or "EO." This seeker type is one of the most difficult seekers to softkill. Fortunately the OSP does have the L50 'Blackjack' Laser Dazzler that is able to blind and stun the seeker type effectively disabling it.
