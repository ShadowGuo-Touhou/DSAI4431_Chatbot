# Missile Defense › Introduction

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
Newer players tend to fall into the trap of "Hardkillmaxxing" where they strap as many point defense turrets to their ships as possible to deter missile threats, or simply don't know the mechanics behind missiles themselves and get frustrated when they die to a more experienced players with complex missile seeker combos.

Newer players often have trouble understanding how to fully protect themselves from missiles, have trouble with APM and situational awareness that allows them to use softkill, or simply prefer to use hardkill because it looks cool.

This guide aims to educate players on the importance of missile defense (specifically, softkill defenses) and how to counter missiles.
