# Beginner's Guide › Starter Fleets

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
It is recommended playing/reviewing the Starter_Fleet_Guides that come with the game. These fleets are community developed to perform incredibly well in multiplayer and give quite a few ideas of optimal ship designs. The community maintainer of the guides are also listed and are frequently active in the Community Discord.
