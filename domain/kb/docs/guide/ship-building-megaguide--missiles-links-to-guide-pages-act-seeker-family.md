## Body
The ACT Seeker family are popular cruise missile seeker combos that can also be used as direct missile seekers. They are characterized by an active radar primary with a validation seeker.

Note, many of the ACT seekers can be replaced with SACT or SEACT depending on the missile maneuverability and range to target for optimal missile strikes.
