## Body
The Ferryman is analogous to ANS' Sprinter, being OSP's least expensive and fastest ship. Unlike the Sprinter, the Ferryman is fairly limited in what roles it can readily fulfill: it will typically act as a rocket-based ambusher, an inexpensive capture asset, an inexpensive skirmisher, or a jamming asset. Despite this, it is still a crucial asset for OSP fleets, and extremely dangerous in the right conditions.

The Draugr is analogous to ANS' Raines, fulfilling the same niche as an overmatch asset against light targets and in being an auxiliary sensors asset, in exchange for losing some versatility overall especially as an escorting asset.

The Monitor is like ANS' Keystone in that it is slow, but unlike the Keystone in most other ways. Where the Keystone struggles in direct combat, the Monitor is designed for frontline combat, taking and dealing damage much in the same way as ANS' Axford and Soloman hulls. When not in a frontline role, Monitors are mostly used for their potentially very generous magazine capacity with missiles, or very occasionally as a defender for a capture point.

As with ANS' Levy, the Journeyman is singularly devoted to launching and using Craft, though it is much lower in cost in exchange for its much lower carrying capacity. While the Journeyman can also do some things in addition to using its craft, it similarly should use these in support of using craft.

The Ocello is directly analogous to ANS' Axford, being a medium weight cruiser which uses 450mm guns and expects to take 450mm gunfire in turn. Its main distinction is as the only OSP ship that may take ANS-unique mounts, enabling it to bring a stronger roster of EWAR and PDT options than any other OSP ship. As a result, the Ocello tends to be employed as an anchor against enemy missile play, while also being one of OSP's strongest frontline combatants.

The Marauder is quite nimble and carries very heavy firepower, in exchange for being extremely large and relatively fragile. Marauders are employed in a wide variety of roles owing to their very generous compartment and mount layout, as well as their aggressive price; missile Marauders, 450mm Marauders, 250mm Marauders, and 100mm/Plasma Marauders are all staples at time of writing.

Like the Journeyman and Levy, the Moorline is fairly restrained in what roles it can fulfill; unlike the two dedicated Carriers, the Moorline gets a choice between two roles instead of one. Moorlines should virtually always be using all four of their uniquely large slots for equipping either large amounts of CM4 container missiles, or for equipping container bank hangars filled with craft. Poor mobility, cost efficiency, and protection mean that the Moorline should spend all its time in the backline, firing cruise missiles or launching craft to remotely effect the game.
