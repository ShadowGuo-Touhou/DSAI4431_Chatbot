## Body
The CMD Seeker family are popular direct missile seeker combos. They use Command seeker as primary with a secondary seeker that enables them to straightrun when they are under Command Jamming. They are reliant on their launching ship's antenna, the more radiated power, the harder it is for Command jamming to affect them. They do require a fire control lock or high enough track quality for them to hit their target properly.
