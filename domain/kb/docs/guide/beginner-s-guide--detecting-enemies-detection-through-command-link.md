## Body
Another option to avoid being jammed is to have another ship, outside of the enemy Radar Jamming detection cone. Either position your ships in such a way that the enemy is unable to jam both ships at once or rely on your team mates to provide radar detection on the jamming ship. If the receiving ship isn't under Command Jamming, it will receive the fire-able tracks on the jamming ship.

To know if your ships are in under a Command Jammer area of influence, you will need to look at the fleet screen in the upper left and look for the Command Jam Icon, as seen below.
