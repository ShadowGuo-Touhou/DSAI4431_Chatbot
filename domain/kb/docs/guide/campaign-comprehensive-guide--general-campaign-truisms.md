# Campaign Comprehensive Guide › General Campaign Truisms

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
- If you lose your flagship Harsh Winds Blow you will lose the campaign

- Knowing how armored a ship you are confronting is and what weapons do well against it is a requirement. It is recommended for beginners to review the Armor page.

- Cooperative play is fully integrated into the campaign, and so long as ships are available, each player can control a ship up to 10 players. This does mean if you are starting to play with friends, the maximum number of players that can join for the first mission is three.

- Prioritize protecting ships over munitions. Expend the necessary missiles or bombers if you’re going to take significant damage. You cannot replace warships in this campaign. There will be opportunities to loot munitions

- The campaign will autosave the start of the mission and the end once the objectives are complete.

- When practical, preserve high-capability missiles for large engagements

- Use the pause button liberally to plan, assess, and reconnoiter the area. The campaign is balanced around this fact

- You can restore ships at stations using the Damage Control Board, which will draw restore components from the station

- Stations will partially restore your ship components without using restores, but they will not restore your armor.

- Stations will generally have fuel, and you can revisit them, but note that revisiting a station will use up fuel for your entire fleet for a reverse trip.

- A knocked-out ship can be salvaged by docking a ship that has at least 4 Restores. The knocked-out ship will only have an engine and a CIC, but can still be moved until a shipyard is reached to repair it

- You can launch Halberd Skiffs to check the ammunition loadout of a disabled enemy ship to see if there is any useful loot

- You can board ships to loot them, and this can be rewarding, but warships can go nuclear during looting
