# Beginner's Guide › Detecting enemies › Burn Through Illuminate

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
A technique to use an illuminator such as a Spotlight Illuminator on a burn through target that is jamming your fleet may be used to offset the jamming interference generated on your fleet.

As can be seen, the jamming artifacts are still present, three of the 5 burn through illuminated targets are visible.
