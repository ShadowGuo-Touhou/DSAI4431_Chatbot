# Beginner's Guide › Testing Ships

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
You may test your ships in one of two ways. The first is by starting a Skirmish and selecting your fleet and the AI fleet (note there is a map called "Small Testing Range 4P" that will have you start within 5km of a target and reduce the time it takes to get into position to test. This will result in an battle with the AI, this is particularly effective in determining if you will be victorious in a gun battle with a certain enemy fleet.

However, if you are determining effectiveness of missile strikes, your own missile defenses, jamming systems, or damage potential of an idle ship a test range Exists for this purpose.

Once you are in a fleet editor, if you hit the "ESC" key there will be an option for the "Testing Range" where you can select a hostile fleet to test your weapons again.

If you wish to test your effectiveness in E-War, it may also be useful to enable control of the enemy ships by using the "dbgEnableEnemyControl True" command in the F2 debug menu that is available in the testing range. For more information, see Console_Commands for more details.

Once you are comfortable understanding the different ships, you may want to look at the Ship_Building_Megaguide to dig into why ships are build the way they are.
