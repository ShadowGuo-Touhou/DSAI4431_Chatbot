# Missile Defense › Introduction › What Exactly IS Hardkill?

Source: Official wiki (wiki.hoodedhorse.com), game 0.6.2.6:260903-1430

## Body
In most nebulous discussions about missile defense, you will commonly hear 2 terms thrown around to describe missile defense: Hardkill and Softkill.

Hardkill is what the name implies, it "hard" kills the threat targeting you. This usually involves the use of ship-board point defense turrets, duel-purpose guns, AMMs, and even craft-based weaponry to destroy targets physically before they can hit you. It is especially popular with newer players to slap everywhere on a ship.
