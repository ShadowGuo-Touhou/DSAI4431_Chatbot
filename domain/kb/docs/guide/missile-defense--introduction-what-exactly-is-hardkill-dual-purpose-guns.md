## Body
Dual-purpose guns are a more longer-ranged supplemental type of point defense, firing long-ranged rounds with decent damage to wean and kill targets. Guns like 250mm with their HE-RFP/Bomb Shell rounds, 120mm with their HE-RPF rounds, and 100mm Grape rounds are great examples of these, being able to reliably kill threats at a longer ranged effectively weaning their numbers. They are weaker towards higher-health and maneuvering targets that "Sarissa-weave", or in other words pivot frequently side-to-side in flight to avoid such point defense.
